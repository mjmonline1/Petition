UK Citizens Deserve Full Transparency in Food Labelling

Consumers should have clear visibility of all pesticide residues and processing aids present in their food — not just a limited subset.

Sign the Official UK Parliament Petition

Why This Matters

Food labelling currently does not require full disclosure of every chemical residue or processing aid that may remain in finished products.

Many consumers actively try to make informed health decisions — but without complete information, true choice is limited.

Transparency is not about fear. It is about clarity, trust, and informed decision-making.
