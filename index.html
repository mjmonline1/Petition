Whether you shop at Waitrose or Lidl, eat organic or not, vote left or right — you deserve to know what you're putting in your body. Right now, UK food labelling law requires producers to list almost everything except pesticide residues and processing aids. This petition asks for one thing: transparency. Nothing more.
